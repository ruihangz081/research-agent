# Delivery Checklist

Before finishing, confirm all items:

- The executive summary matches the body and contains no new fact.
- Every material number retains a deterministic evidence citation or an explicit source note.
- Every chart placeholder has one unique manifest entry and every required manifest entry is used.
- No ASCII chart, “建议插入图表”, raw drawing code, or unsafe chart resource remains.
- Chart titles, units, dates, sources, labels, values, and actual/forecast markers are complete.
- Tables have headers, consistent units, source notes, and manageable width.
- Headings use no more than three levels and chapter takeaways do not duplicate the summary.
- Risks include observable triggers and do not read as generic boilerplate.
- Data gaps remain visible; no missing value has been silently inferred.
- The traceable evidence index, risk disclosure, automation disclosure, and disclaimer are present.
