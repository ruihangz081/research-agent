# Report Structure

Use this order unless the approved outline requires a clearly better domain-specific sequence:

1. Cover information: report title, topic, date, version, and automation disclosure.
2. Executive summary: three to five conclusion-led bullets plus a 300-500 Chinese-character synthesis.
3. Key findings dashboard: the most decision-relevant verified metrics in a compact table.
4. Main research chapters: begin each chapter with two or three takeaways, then evidence, interpretation, and implications.
5. Outlook or scenarios: clearly distinguish historical actuals, estimates, and assumptions.
6. Risks: state trigger, transmission path, affected conclusion, and observable indicator.
7. Conclusions and actions: connect recommendations to evidence and constraints.
8. Traceable evidence index and source appendix.
9. Methodology, risk disclosure, and disclaimer.

Keep headings conclusion-led where possible. Use at most three heading levels. Avoid repeating the executive summary in later chapters. Preserve the approved outline's audience, scope, and required deliverables.
