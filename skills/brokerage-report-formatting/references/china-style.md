# Chinese Brokerage Style

- Write in concise, evidence-led Simplified Chinese.
- Use full-width Chinese punctuation in prose and consistent Arabic numerals for data.
- Distinguish `实际值`, `预测值`, and `情景假设` explicitly.
- Do not issue a securities rating unless the approved research scope and verified evidence support one. Otherwise use neutral research conclusions.
- Do not imply guaranteed returns or remove material uncertainty.
- State data cutoff dates and source definitions.
- End with a visible risk disclosure and the disclaimer: “本报告基于公开信息和用户授权材料自动整理，仅供研究参考，不构成任何投资建议。”
- Refer to the system as `Research Agent` in automation disclosures; do not impersonate a licensed brokerage or reuse a brokerage trademark.
